# Update 1.12 (Codename: Mango) — New Features

This document tracks all new features, architectural changes, schema migrations, and improvements introduced in the 1.12 Mango update cycle.

### v1.12.3-Mango (2026-10-12)
- **Separate Full-Page View for Order Creation & Editing (`src/components/OrderDetailView.tsx`, `src/components/OrdersView.tsx`)**:
  - Eliminated the popup modal dialog in favor of a dedicated full-page view when creating or editing an order.
  - Implemented the master dual-panel layout matching Leads (`grid grid-cols-1 ws-lg:grid-cols-12 gap-6 items-start`):
    - **Left Panel (`ws-lg:col-span-5 space-y-6`)**: Customer relationship card (toggle between registered CRM client and guest checkout, searchable client selector with synced card, avatar initials, contact, email, phone, IČO/DIČ, direct client profile link, address sync), delivery address & carrier configuration (DPD, Packeta, GLS, SPS, personal pickup, tracking code with quick copy & carrier web tracker bridge), billing address with 1-click "Same as delivery" sync, and payment method, due date, warehouse fulfillment selector & internal team notes.
    - **Right Panel (`ws-lg:col-span-7 space-y-6`)**: Interactive order items table, searchable item combobox dropdown, quick service presets (courier shipping, cash on delivery fee, assembly, custom items), and live financial calculation panel (products net, services net, tax base, VAT 20%, total payable amount).
- **Searchable Dropdown for Adding Items**:
  - Searchable combobox dropdown allowing real-time searching and filtering through warehouse inventory by item name, SKU, barcode, and category.
  - Displays SKU tags, stock level indicators, and unit price with automatic tax and line total computation upon selection.
- **View-Size Standards & Clean Architecture**:
  - Strict compliance with `docs/VIEW-SIZE.md` tokens (`text-ui`, `type-overline`, `text-body`, `text-title`, `type-entity-title`), zero hardcoded font sizes, and responsive workspace container query tokens (`ws-lg:grid-cols-12`, `ws-lg:col-span-5`, `ws-lg:col-span-7`).

### v1.12.2-Mango (2026-10-12)
- **Procedural Chinese Landscape Painting Infinite Background (`public/shanshui.html`, `src/components/ShanShuiBackground.tsx`, `src/components/LoginView.tsx`)**:
  - Integrated the authentic procedural Chinese landscape art generation library (`LingDong-/shan-shui-inf`) into the login page.
  - Procedurally generates layered mountains, textured pine trees, pagodas, bridges, boats, and rippling water atop a seamless 4-way symmetrical noise-textured rice paper (宣纸) parchment.
  - Infinite right-to-left scrolling runtime: camera shifts rightwards at a meditative pace (~24 coordinate pixels/sec), continuously streaming fresh terrain into view from the right.
  - Zero-leak chunk pruning: terrain chunks and noise coordinates scrolling past the left viewport boundary are automatically pruned from SVG and memory, maintaining constant $O(1)$ memory consumption indefinitely.
  - Full-screen responsive viewport adaptation: calculates dynamic aspect-ratio SVG viewBox geometry on load and resize, ensuring crisp rendering across ultrawide monitors, laptops, and mobile screens.
  - Preserved login card and digital clock contrast with frosted glass backdrop panes (`bg-white/75`, `backdrop-blur-md`) and deep ink slate typography.

### v1.12.1-Mango (2026-10-12)
- **E-Shop Orders Engine (`src/components/OrdersView.tsx`, `api/orders.php`, `public/api/orders.php`, `src/types/index.ts`, `src/utils/orders.ts`)**:
  - Full commercial and e-shop order lifecycle management supporting both registered CRM clients and guest checkouts.
  - Dedicated delivery and billing address structures with recipient, street, city, ZIP, country, and carrier tracking details.
  - Separate item categorization for warehouse products vs additional services (e.g. Courier Shipping, Cash on Delivery).
  - Live financial totals computation with tax calculation, goods net, services net, and total payable amount.
  - Quick action to convert guest customer accounts into full CRM clients in 1-click.
  - Quick warehouse stock dispatch (výdajka) and invoicing bridge triggers.
- **Customizable Order Statuses in System Settings (`src/components/OrderStatusSettings.tsx`, `src/components/SettingsView.tsx`, `src/hooks/useOrderStatuses.tsx`)**:
  - Configurable status workflow with customizable names, hex colors, and lifecycle stages (`new`, `processing`, `shipped`, `delivered`, `cancelled`).
  - Stored within `system_settings` (`s.orderStatuses`) and synchronized across clients with zero database migration friction.
- **Client Detail Order History Tab (`src/components/ClientsView.tsx`)**:
  - Added "Orders" (`ShoppingCart`) tab to the client detail view showing total order count badge.
  - History of all orders linked to the active client with real-time status badges, item counts, tracking numbers, and payment statuses.
- **Database Schema & Delta Sync Engine (`api/schema.php`, `sync.php`, `src/App.tsx`)**:
  - Added SQLite/MySQL migrations for `orders` and `order_items` tables with indices on `client_id`, `status`, and `order_number`.
  - Added delta sync protocol v2 support in `sync.php` and `src/App.tsx` with identity-hashed baselines and automatic server synchronization.
- **Navigation & RBAC Permissions (`src/utils/permissions.ts`, `src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `src/components/StartMenu.tsx`, `src/components/VisibleModulesSettings.tsx`)**:
  - Registered `orders` permission section with granular read/create/edit/delete access control.
  - Added Orders module icon and routing aliases (`#orders`, `#order`, `#objednavky`, `#rendelesek`).
