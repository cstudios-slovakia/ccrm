### v1.8.6-Imbe (2026-08-18)
* **Hold-to-Unlock Protection for Fixed Product Data**:
  * Added a security & safety **Lock/Unlock mechanism** to the Left Sidebar Card in [src/components/WarehouseView.tsx](file:///Users/erik/Documents/vibe%20coding/crm/src/components/WarehouseView.tsx).
  * **Hold-to-Unlock Interaction**:
    * To prevent accidental modifications to fixed product attributes (Name, Photo, Suggested Sale Price, EAN/Barcode, SKU, Category, Unit, Location, Expiration, Description), the card defaults to a locked state for existing catalog items.
    * Unlocking requires pressing and **holding the lock button for 1 second** (`1000ms`), featuring a live filling progress animation overlay and percentage countdown. Releasing before 1 second cancels the unlock action.
    * Once unlocked, fields become editable, and clicking the button instantly re-locks the fixed data.
  * Added disabled states, locked styling, and toast notifications on successful unlock.
