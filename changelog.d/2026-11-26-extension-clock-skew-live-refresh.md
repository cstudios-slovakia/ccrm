- **Chrome extension: starting a timer no longer fails on a fast PC clock** (`public/api/time_tracker_lib.php`): the server refused any start or stop time even a millisecond ahead of its own clock ("This computer's clock differs too much from the server"), so a PC clock a second ahead broke every ▶ click. A client time up to 5 minutes ahead is now clamped to the server's now; further ahead is still refused.
- **Chrome extension side panel follows the app** (`extensions/chrome-time-tracker/src/liveRefresh.ts`, `popup.ts`): the open panel re-reads the running timers every 10 s and re-renders when they changed, so a timer started, stopped or paused in the app shows up without reopening the panel. It never re-renders while you type, edit an entry or have settings open. Extension version 2.0.1.
