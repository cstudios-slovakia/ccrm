- **Time tracking: the presence engine** (`public/api/time_presence_lib.php`, `time_entries.php`, `time_tracker_lib.php`, `time_tracker_ext.php`, `mcp_ext_timers.php`, `src/utils/timeTrackingApi.ts`): one presence cycle per person, owned by the server. 50 minutes after the last answer the person is asked; with no answer 10 minutes later every running timer ends at the moment of the question and is paused, and the pause can be resumed for another 10 minutes. `autoStopAt` finally works: a timer still running at that company time ends there. Starting, resuming, stopping one of several or editing a running entry counts as an answer; `POST action=confirm` is the explicit one (signed in only). The result is computed from timestamps, so it is the same whichever request or cron notices first. `?running=1` now returns the `presence` state.
- **Tests**: the time tracking probe runs on a controllable clock (`CCRM_TT_TEST_CLOCK` + `TT_TEST_NOW`, probe only) and covers the answer at minute 55, the automatic pause, resume, expiry, lazy vs cron and `autoStopAt` (T20).
