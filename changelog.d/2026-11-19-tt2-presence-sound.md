- **Time tracking presence sound** (`presenceSound.ts`, `usePresenceSound.ts`, `PresenceSoundBlock.tsx`): while the "still working?" question is open exactly one tab plays a chime (every 20 s, then every 8 s in the second half of the window), stops on the answer or the pause; each person sets volume, mute and one of three sounds in Settings -> Time tracking. The dialog and notification cannot be turned off.
