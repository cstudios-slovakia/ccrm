- **Lead pipeline strip stays readable with many states** (`src/utils/leadPipeline.ts`, `src/components/ui/PipelineStrip.tsx`, `pipelineCollapse.ts`, `leads/LeadPipelineRowStrip.tsx`): the strip in the lead drawer and under each row of the leads list shows only main states. A substate lights its parent, which then reads "Parent › Substate". A state counts as closed by its stage group, not its English name, so a Slovak "Odmietnutá" ends the pipeline instead of sitting in the middle of it. All closed states share one end segment: "Closed" while the lead is open, the actual outcome in its colour once the lead is closed. With more than 7 steps, or labels that would not fit, the strip folds around the current step (`✓ 6 · current · next · +3 · outcome`), and hovering a folded segment lists the steps it hides. The project strip folds the same way. The drawer and the list rows now share one segment builder instead of two inline copies.
- **Settings → Pipeline stages: "Pipeline bar" switch per state** (`settings/LeadStatePipelineToggle.tsx`, `public/sync.php`, setting `LEAD_STATE_PIPELINE_HIDDEN`): turn it off for states that are not a sales step (for example "Do not contact"), and they leave the strip. A lead sitting in such a state still sees it as its current step. Substates and closed states get no switch, because they are never steps of their own.
