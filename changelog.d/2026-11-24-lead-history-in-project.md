- **The paired lead's history on the project timeline** (`utils/leadHistoryTimeline.ts`, `projects/LeadHistoryTimeline.tsx`, `ProjectDetailsView.tsx`): a project paired with a lead (also one converted from it) shows the lead's timeline entries (calls, e-mails, offers, status changes, notes) woven in by time as read-only rows marked "Lead". Nothing is copied: the entries are read from the lead, so they stay current and projects paired earlier get them too. A "Show lead history (N)" switch above the list hides them.
