- **Newsletter sending engine and public pages** (`public/api/newsletter_send.php`, `newsletter_mailer.php`, `newsletter_merge.php`, `newsletter_public.php`, `newsletter.php`, `cron.php`): the actions `send_test`, `start_send`, `pause`, `resume`, `cancel` and `tick`. Starting a send checks the subject, the mail profile, the unsubscribe link, `data:` images and `javascript:` links, then freezes the audience into one queue row per address with a private token. A worker (cron every run, or the open campaign page) sends in throttled batches over one SMTP session with the system mail profile, retries a temporary failure up to three times, pauses the campaign with the server's message when login or connection fails, adds an address to the suppression list when its mailbox does not exist, and marks the campaign sent when the queue is empty. Every message carries `List-Unsubscribe` with one-click, an RFC 2047 subject and quoted-printable text and HTML parts. In demo mode no connection is opened and rows are simply marked sent. The public page `newsletter_public.php` shows and confirms the unsubscribe (a GET never unsubscribes), answers the one-click POST, shows the e-mail in the browser and handles test and unknown links, in the system language.
- **Tests**: `scripts/test-newsletter.php` now also runs the 44 shared merge-tag cases against the PHP port, the MIME builder (headers, encoded subject, quoted-printable line length, dot-stuffing), the SMTP client against a scripted server, the send validations and the pure parts of the public pages.
