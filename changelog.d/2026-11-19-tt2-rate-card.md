- **Time tracking: monthly cost rates follow paid leave** (`public/api/time_rate_card.php`, `time_rate_months_migration.php`, `time_entries_lib.php`, `time_entries.php`, `cron.php`, `entities_overview.md`): a person paid monthly now gets an hourly cost rate from the days they could actually work: approved or taken paid leave raises it, unpaid leave lowers the month's cost, and their own hours per day apply. Each month's rate is kept in a new rate card (`time_rate_months`). While the month is open it is re-priced automatically when salary, leave, holidays or hours change (also by a daily cron pass), with one audit-log line. It freezes when the month's payroll record is closed, or at the end of the following month; after that only the admin "Re-price a period" recomputes it.
- **Tests**: the time tracking server probe gets T16 (leave booked after tracking re-prices the open month once; a closed payroll month is not re-priced until the admin re-prices it), and T2 now expects an open month to follow a calendar change.
