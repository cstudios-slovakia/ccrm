- **Time tracking presence, server pass** (`api/time_presence_cron.php`, `api/cron.php`, `api/time_entries.php`, `api/time_entries_lib.php`): the cron now settles every user with a timer every minute, so the automatic pause, its expiry and the auto-stop time happen even when nobody has the app open, and it sends the presence pushes (the question once a minute, "Paused at", "Timers stopped at"); answering on one device sends a silent push that closes the question on the others. The `presence` settings (question after, confirm and resume windows, trim to question or deadline) are defaulted and validated on the server, and Settings learns when the cron last ran so it can warn when it is not running. Timers stopped from the browser extension or through MCP now record how they ended.
