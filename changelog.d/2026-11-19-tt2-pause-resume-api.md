- **Time tracking: pause and resume in the API** (`public/api/time_pause_migration.php`, `time_pause_lib.php`, `time_entries.php`, `time_entries_lib.php`, `src/utils/timeTrackingApi.ts`, `entities_overview.md`): `time_entries` gets `paused_at`, `pause_reason`, `resume_until`, `continues_id` and `end_reason`. `POST pause {id | all}` ends the running item(s) and keeps a resumable marker; `POST resume {id | all}` starts a new entry that continues the paused one (409 `pause_expired` once the day's auto-stop time or `resume_until` has passed); stop on a paused item clears its marker. Entries carry `state` (ongoing, paused, stopped) and `endReason`; `GET ?running=1` fills `paused`. Stop sets `end_reason = user`, a switch-start `switch`. No UI yet (TT2 phase 6a).
