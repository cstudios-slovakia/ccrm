- **Leads list: a small line under a lead's name tells same-name leads apart** (`src/utils/leadClientLabel.ts`, `src/components/LeadClientLabel.tsx`, `LeadsDatagrid.tsx`): in the table, the cards and the kanban board, a lead linked to a client shows the client's name when it differs from the lead's own (a lead filed under a person, e.g. from a web form without a company), or when several leads share a name but belong to different clients. A lead named exactly like its only client gets no label, so the list is not doubled. A lead with no client yet shows its own e-mail instead.
