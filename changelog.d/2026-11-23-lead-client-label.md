- **Leads list: the client's name under a lead when it tells two leads apart** (`src/utils/leadClientLabel.ts`, `src/components/LeadClientLabel.tsx`, `LeadsDatagrid.tsx`): a lead filed under a person (a web form without a company, or a lead created for an existing client under another name) shows its client's name in small type under its own, in the table, the cards and the kanban board. The label also appears when several leads share a name but belong to different clients. A lead named exactly like its only client gets no label, so the list is not doubled.
