- **Existing roles no longer get the Agency module by default** (`src/utils/permissions.ts`, `public/api/permissions.php`): the Agency & Workforce Leasing permissions (`agency`, `agency.delete`, `agency.personal`) took the default for a role that has never stored them, which is "edit", so on an install that updates into the module every existing role (Employee included) would have opened and edited agency workers and their personal data, and their AI agents would have seen the agency tools. They now default to "nothing" for such a role, as WhatsApp and Newsletter do; an admin grants them in Settings → Roles. Admins and the built-in presets that list the keys keep their access.
- **Tests**: a stored role without the agency keys resolves every agency key to "nothing" (`src/utils/permissions.test.ts`); `test:mcp` checks that a role with nothing, or with view only, sees no agency tool.
