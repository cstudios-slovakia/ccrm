- **Time tracking: the Chrome extension shows every timer and asks "Still working?"** (TT2 phase 10a; `public/api/time_tracker_ext.php`, new `time_tracker_ext_presence.php`, `time_tracker_lib.php`, `extensions/chrome-time-tracker/`, probe T24): the popup lists every running timer with its own elapsed time and Stop button under today's worked clock, and lists paused items with Resume and Stop. When the presence question is due, the popup shows "Still working?" with a countdown and a **Still working** button (answered in the popup, never from a notification). After the deadline it shows "Paused at 09:50". The badge shows an amber "?" during the question and "II" while paused. With the new `idle` permission, a locked or idle computer shows "paused" as soon as the deadline passes, without waiting for the next poll, and the server is read again when you come back. Server: the extension `running` call adds `presence` and `paused[]`; new device actions `confirm` (409 `paused` after the deadline) and `resume {id}` (always a switch); `stop {id}` on a paused item clears its pause. Extension version 1.1.0; the download zip is rebuilt.
