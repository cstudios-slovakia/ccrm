- **Contact first and last name, backend** (`schema.php`, `sync.php`, `contact_name.php`): a client or lead keeps its contact person's first and last name in two new fields (`contact_first_name`, `contact_last_name`, synced as `contactFirstName` / `contactLastName`). The full contact person stays and is composed from the two (given name first). Existing contacts are split once where that is safe (two or more words, last word is the last name); one-word names, names with a comma or a trailing title stay empty for a person to complete. The AI assistant tools, the web form, voice copilot, automations and guest-order conversion keep the two parts in step with the full name.
- **Newsletter**: `{{first_name}}` of a client or lead is the stored first name with titles removed (`Ing. Michal` becomes `Michal`) and is never guessed from the full name; pasted extra addresses have none. The audience count reports how many addresses have no first name.
- **Demo data**: every demo contact has a first and last name (Hungarian: given name in the first name).
- **Demo wipe**: wiping the demo no longer deletes a user's own newsletter recipient rows that point at a demo lead or employee, and the row count is no longer doubled.
