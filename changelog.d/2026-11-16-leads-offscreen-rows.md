- **A long leads list no longer stutters on hover** (`src/hooks/useHideOffscreenRows.ts`, `LeadsDatagrid.tsx`, `index.css`): rows far outside the visible part of the page are no longer painted (they stay in place and keep their size, so the layout and scrolling do not change; a row is painted again before it comes within 900 px of the screen). Every frame used to cost time for all rows on the page: on a 524-row list a row hover, with its stage strip growing, drew about 6 frames in 0.9 s; with the far rows unpainted it draws about 20, and the page idles at 60 frames per second. The browser's own find-in-page does not see the unpainted rows; the list search does.
