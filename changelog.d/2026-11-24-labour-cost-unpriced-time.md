- **Project labour cost no longer disappears when one time entry has no cost**: an entry without a cost rate (unpaid time, an employee without a rate yet) now simply counts as no cost, so the project keeps the labour cost of its other entries instead of dropping to zero. The Overview's profitability card no longer warns about this; its "costs are not visible to you" note stays only for people without the time cost permission. The project's labour line and the project Time tab say how much tracked time has no cost rate and is not included.
