- **MCP: overdue money in the cash-flow forecast, and no silent caps** (`get_cash_flow_forecast`, `get_client_invoices`, `get_project_billing_summary`): the forecast gains an `overdue` block (unpaid issued movements dated before today, with income / expense / net totals, items oldest first, `truncated`), plus `planned_past` and `due_today` counts and `as_of`; `months` is unchanged. `get_client_invoices` returns `truncated` when a source holds more than its 300-row cap, and `get_project_billing_summary` takes `offset` and returns `truncated`.
