- **Newsletter: the e-mail builder, sending and results are in the app** (`src/components/newsletter/`): the design route `#newsletter/<id>/design` opens the builder full page with the locked global header and footer, autosave (1.5 s debounce, flush on leave) and Send test; the campaign's Content card shows a live thumbnail, Edit design and the header/footer switches (with an unsubscribe warning); the Review and send card has the checklist, renderer warnings, Send test and Hold to send (posts the HTML and text from `renderEmail()`); started campaigns show progress, time left, Pause / Resume / Cancel, the last error, View e-mail and a paged recipients table, polling `tick` every 5 s while sending and the tab is visible. Settings › Newsletter › Design has the header and footer editors (`settings/newsletter/design/header|footer`).
- **Newsletter errors are translated**: `newsletterErrorMessage` maps every known server code (including the send errors) to a Slovak, Hungarian or English line and uses the server's English `message` only for an unknown code; an SMTP failure shows the server's reply as a detail.
- **Tests**: `reviewChecklist.test.ts` (checklist, send-block reason, time-left estimate, poll rule).
