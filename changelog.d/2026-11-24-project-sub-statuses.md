- **Project sub-statuses** (`utils/projectStatusTree.ts`, `utils/projects.ts`, `ProjectStatusSettings.tsx`, `projects/useProjectFacets.ts`, `ui/CustomSelect.tsx`, `api/auth.php`): like lead sub-states, a project status can sit under a main status (for example "Goods partly delivered 1…5" under "Goods delivered"). Settings → Project statuses has a "Main status / Under …" picker per row; a sub-status takes its parent's group, is listed indented right under it, and leaves the parent when dragged into another group. Status pickers show sub-statuses indented, and in the projects status filter picking a main status also shows (and counts) its sub-statuses. Stored in the existing `PROJECT_STATUSES` setting (`parent` key), one level deep.
