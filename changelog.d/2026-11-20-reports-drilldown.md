- **Reports: click a row to split it** (`TimeReportsView.tsx`, `ReportSummaryTable.tsx`, `ReportFilters.tsx`, `timeReports.ts`): in the Summary every group row opens to its employees (or tasks, projects, clients, days), with the same columns and margin rule, so the parts add up to the row. The "Group by" chip now reads e.g. "Project → Employee" and the second level can be switched off. A notice appears when a period has 5,000 or more entries, because the server returns no more than that and the oldest are missing.
