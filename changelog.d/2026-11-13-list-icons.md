- **Icons in front of list labels** (`src/components/ui/ListIconPicker.tsx`, `ListIcon.tsx`, `src/utils/listIcons.ts`, `listIconStore.ts`, `public/api/list_icons.php`): lead sources, divisions, interest categories, price-offer categories and income/expense categories can each carry a small picture, picked next to the colour in Settings and shown in front of the label in the leads list, the lead drawer, the offers list and filter, and the finance ledger. Made for flags: a ".sk" source gets 🇸🇰 and a ".hu" source 🇭🇺, as in the customer's old system. The 119 icons are Twemoji (CC BY 4.0) drawn as SVG from one cached sprite (`public/list-icons.svg`, generated by `scripts/list-icons/build-list-icons.mjs`), never emoji characters, because Windows shows a flag emoji as two letters. Business pictures only, no faces. The icons are one setting (`LIST_ICONS`) saved by their own endpoint, one change at a time, so picking one never sends the whole dataset; a rename carries the icon along.
- **Tests**: `listIcons.test.ts` (keys, set/clear, rename, unknown ids, catalogue), `tests/e2e/listIcons.spec.ts` (flag in the Settings chip and the leads list, picking and clearing saves just that change).
