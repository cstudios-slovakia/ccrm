- **Figures that would leave out a hidden labour cost are no longer shown**: without the time cost permission, a project whose tracked time has a cost you may not see now shows a dash instead of costs, profit and margin, both on the Overview profitability card (the project keeps its invoiced amount; the totals row follows) and in the project's Finances (costs, net profit, margin, the Finances badges and the budget bar), with a short note explaining why. The old "labour cost not included" warning is gone. In the task drawer, a person without a cost rate now counts as no cost like on projects: the task total sums the known costs and says how much time has no rate.
