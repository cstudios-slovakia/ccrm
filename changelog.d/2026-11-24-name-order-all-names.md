- **Name order follows the company language for every first + last name** (`src/utils/nameOrder.ts`, `employeeIdentity.ts`, `EmployeeIdentityFields.tsx`, `CandidatesPipelineView.tsx`, `public/api/contact_name.php`, `sync.php`, `mcp.php`, `mcp_ext_crm_plus.php`, `mcp_ext_writes.php`, `newsletter_audience.php`): in Hungarian a new employee or freelancer is composed as "Surname Given" and the form shows the surname field first, a new agency candidate likewise (it was always surname first, now given name first in the other languages), and the MCP contact tools and the newsletter's employee names compose in the same order. An employee's stored name is the key task owners, project managers and time tracking match, and nothing renames those references, so it is never rewritten: it keeps its text while the two parts are unchanged, and an edited part is rewritten in the order the name already had (`composeKeyedPersonName` / `ccrm_compose_keyed_person_name`). Changing the company language therefore reorders contact names only, not employees.
