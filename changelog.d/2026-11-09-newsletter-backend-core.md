- **Newsletter backend core** (`public/api/newsletter.php`, `newsletter_audience.php`, `newsletter_lib.php`, `schema.php`): the three tables `newsletter_campaigns`, `newsletter_recipients` and `newsletter_suppressions`, and an endpoint for campaigns (list, get, create, update drafts, duplicate, soft delete), the recipient list, the live audience count, the suppression list, the `NEWSLETTER_CONFIG` settings with defaults and validation, and image upload. The audience resolves clients (with their linked leads, by category and sub-category or by client), optional leads, employees and pasted extra addresses, removes duplicates (client before lead before employee before extra) and skips unsubscribed and invalid addresses. Sending comes in a later change.
- **Tests**: `scripts/test-newsletter.php` (`npm run test:newsletter`): address parsing, first-name title stripping, dedupe precedence, suppression skipping, client profiles, settings clamping.
