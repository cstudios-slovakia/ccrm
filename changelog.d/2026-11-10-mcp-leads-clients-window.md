- **MCP `list_leads`, `get_lead`, `list_clients`, `get_client`, `get_client_profile`, `list_contacts` answer "what changed since X?"** (`public/api/mcp_lists_crm.php`, `mcp.php`, `mcp_ext_ops.php`, `mcp_ext_writes.php`): `updated_at` is returned as an ISO moment with the app's offset and takes `updated_from`/`updated_to` and `sort_by=updated_at` (stable id tiebreak, `offset`). `list_leads` runs the leads/clients module split in SQL so pages are full, takes `include_archived` (rows carry `archived`), `created_from`/`created_to` as a date range (`leads.created_at` is a date), and `last_activity_at` plus `activity_from`/`activity_to`, which also see timeline entries (a timeline entry does not move `updated_at`, by design). A client profile's `updated_at` is the newest of its records. `list_contacts` honours the module split. `get_lead_pipeline_summary` applies the module split and states its `as_of` date.
