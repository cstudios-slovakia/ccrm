- **MCP: invoices show their payment state** (`list_invoices`, `get_invoice`): each invoice carries `payment_status` (paid, partially_paid, unpaid, overdue, planned, cancelled; overdue is derived from the due date, as in `get_client_invoices`), `paid`, `open`, `paid_date` and `status_changed_at`. New filters: `payment_status`, `due_from/to`, `issued_from/to`, `activity_from/to`, so "which invoices are overdue across all clients, and how much is open" is one call. A payment does not move the invoice's `updated_at`; it moves `last_activity_at` (the later of the invoice and its ledger movement), which can be filtered and sorted on.
- **MCP: ledger overdue is derived** (`list_financial_movements`): `status=overdue` now means a one-off movement that is not paid, cancelled or planned and is flagged overdue or past its due date; rows carry `payment_status`. `list_recurring_movements` returns `amount_history`.
