- **Contact names in Hungarian order** (`src/utils/nameOrder.ts`, `contactName.ts`, `ui/ContactNameFields.tsx`, `api/contact_name.php`): when the system language is Hungarian, a lead's or client's contact name is composed surname first ("Kovács János"), a full name is split that way, and the form shows the surname field first. Existing contact names whose parts are filled are rewritten to the company's order on update and whenever the system language changes. Employee names are unchanged.
