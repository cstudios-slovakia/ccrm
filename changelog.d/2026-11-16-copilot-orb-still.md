- **The floating copilot orb no longer slows every screen** (`src/components/executive/FloatingCopilotOrb.tsx`): the orb's avatar keeps breathing, bobbing and blinking, but its eyes glance around only while the pointer is over it. Those two eye animations made the browser lay the page out again on every frame; on a long list (the leads pipeline with about 500 rows) each frame took 200-300 ms, so hovering rows and expanding panels stuttered. Measured on such a list: 15-30 frames per second before, 60 with the two animations paused.
