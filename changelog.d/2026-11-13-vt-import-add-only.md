- **Villatesta importer: add-only cutover** (`scripts/import/villatesta/load.php --add-only [--skip-leads=]`, `lib.php`): a second load into a CRM people already work in creates only the clients, leads, projects, users and mail it does not hold yet, adds the history existing records gained in the old CRM, and never changes an existing row. A new record whose e-mail or phone matches one entered in the CRM itself (by hand or by the website webhook) is passed over and what pointed at it is re-pointed. The report lists everything created or passed over and every lead whose status, owner or archive flag differs between the two systems. Rehearsed on a scratch database with a synthetic dataset (existing fields and history kept, duplicate and test passed over, a re-run adds nothing).
