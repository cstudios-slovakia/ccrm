- **MCP: employees, salaries, vacations, stock, workflows and registries answer "what changed" and "what is due"** (`public/api/mcp_lists_people.php`, `mcp.php`, `mcp_ext_ops.php`, `mcp_ext.php`): `list_employees`, `list_salaries`, `list_vacations`, `list_team_members`, `list_inventory_items`, `list_stock_movements`, `list_batches` and `list_suppliers` take the shared change window (`created_from/_to`, `updated_from/_to`, `sort_by`, `sort_order`, `limit`, `offset`, an id tiebreak) and return `created_at` / `updated_at` as ISO 8601 moments with the app's offset. `get_employee`, `get_inventory_item`, `list_workflows` (`last_run`, `updated_at`), `get_workflow_runs`, `list_client_categories` and `list_financial_categories` stop returning raw database timestamps, and `list_salaries` returns `closed_at` as ISO.
- **MCP: business-date ranges**: `list_vacations` takes an overlap range `from`/`to`; `get_vacation_balance` takes `on_date` (who is on leave that day, `on_leave`); `list_salaries` takes `due_from`/`due_to` and `payment_from`/`payment_to`; `list_stock_movements` `from`/`to` take a time of day; `get_unified_entry_rows` takes `due_from`/`due_to` and a `bucket` (overdue/today/tomorrow/upcoming) for registries with a due date, and now has a deterministic order, `offset`, and a change window.
- **MCP: paging and "today"**: `list_employees` (200), `list_salaries` (500) and `list_batches` (200) page past their old caps with `offset`; `list_vacations` gets a limit (default 1000) and `offset`; `list_inventory_items`, `list_suppliers`, `list_stock_movements` and `get_workflow_runs` get `offset`. `list_batches` (`expiring_within_days`) and `get_vacation_balance` use the app's date instead of the MySQL server's `CURDATE()`.
