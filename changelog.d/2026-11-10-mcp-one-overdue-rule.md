- **MCP money tools share one overdue rule, and documents without a ledger movement are no receivables** (`public/api/mcp_ext_intel.php`, `mcp_lists_money.php`): a movement or invoice payment is overdue when it is unpaid (not paid, cancelled or planned) and flagged overdue or dated before today (due date, else issue date). `payment_status`, `list_financial_movements status=overdue`, `list_invoices`, `get_project` installments and the forecast overdue block all use it (`mcp_is_overdue`, `mcp_overdue_date_sql`); the forecast block's `basis` now says what it holds and its months are unchanged. A proforma or a rejected invoice with no movement reports `payment_status` null and `open` 0; `sort_by=due_date` lists undated invoices, expenses and movements last in both directions.
