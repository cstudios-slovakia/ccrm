- **Projects: acceptance criteria and scope approval** (`src/components/projects/ProjectAcceptanceCriteria.tsx`, `src/utils/acceptanceCriteria.ts`, `public/sync.php`, `public/api/project_scope.php`, `public/api/schema.php`): every project has a new "Acceptance criteria" tab. Add, rename, tick and remove criteria, then approve the scope once. A criterion added after the approval is marked "Added after approval" and counts as a scope extension; withdrawing the approval asks for confirmation first. Withdrawing the approval is a reset: when the scope is approved again, the criteria added so far become part of the original scope. Removed criteria are kept in the history. Who added or removed a criterion and when, and who approved the scope and when, is stamped by the server, not by the browser, and a stale browser tab cannot delete or bring back a criterion. A new project can get criteria only after it is saved. Read-only users see the list and the approval line. Three new nullable columns on `projects` (`acceptance_criteria_json`, `scope_approved_at`, `scope_approved_by`) with migration, sync column healing and `entities_overview.md`; a client that does not send them leaves the stored values alone.
- **Performance overview: Scope creep on real data** (`src/utils/performance/scopeCreep.ts`, `src/components/dashboard/performance/`): the widget now counts the criteria added in the period to projects with an approved scope, their share of the original scope and the five projects with the most additions, instead of its empty state. A project without a name of its own is now shown under its paired client's name (not its id) in the Project profitability, Project deadlines, Task throughput, Rework count and Scope creep widgets.
