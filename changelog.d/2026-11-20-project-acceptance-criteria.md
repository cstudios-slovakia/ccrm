- **Projects: acceptance criteria and scope approval** (`src/components/projects/ProjectAcceptanceCriteria.tsx`, `src/utils/acceptanceCriteria.ts`, `public/sync.php`, `public/api/project_scope.php`, `public/api/schema.php`): every project has a new "Acceptance criteria" tab. Add, rename, tick and remove criteria, then approve the scope once. A criterion added after the approval is marked "Added after approval" and counts as a scope extension; withdrawing the approval asks for confirmation first. Removed criteria are kept in the history. Read-only users see the list and the approval line. Three new nullable columns on `projects` (`acceptance_criteria_json`, `scope_approved_at`, `scope_approved_by`) with migration, sync column healing and `entities_overview.md`; a client that does not send them leaves the stored values alone.
- **Performance overview: Scope creep on real data** (`src/utils/performance/scopeCreep.ts`, `src/components/dashboard/performance/`): the widget now counts the criteria added in the period to projects with an approved scope, their share of the original scope and the five projects with the most additions, instead of its empty state.
