- **Time tracking: amounts are hidden until you click the eye, and fixed-salary employees see none**: every money sum in a time tracking section (the Labour cost tile, day totals, entry rows, the entry drawer, Timesheet, Reports, Team, Salary report) shows `•••••` until the eye button in the toolbar is clicked; one click reveals all amounts of that section, and switching section or person hides them again. An employee paid a fixed monthly (or daily) salary no longer sees labour cost on their own time at all, since it does not change with the hours they track; contractors and hourly pay still do, and people with the costs permission still see everyone's.
