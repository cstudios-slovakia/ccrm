- **MCP `get_client_dossier` separates upcoming from past meetings** (`public/api/mcp_ext_intel.php`): "upcoming meetings" used to be the last five meetings, past ones included. It now returns `upcoming_meetings` (dated today or later, soonest first, at most 5) and `recent_meetings` (already past, newest first), the timeline with `timestamp_iso`, `profile.updated_at`, and `open_tasks_truncated` when the 50-task cap cut the list.
