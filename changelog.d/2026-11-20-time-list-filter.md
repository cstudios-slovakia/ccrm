- **Time tracking: List filter** (`TimeListFilter.tsx`, `TimeListView.tsx`, `TimeToolbarSlot.tsx`, `src/utils/timeListFilter.ts`): a funnel button at the end of the toolbar row on the List tab filters the shown week by project (or no project), billable and tag, in the browser over the entries already loaded. Day cards, day totals and grouped rows follow the filter, the stat tiles keep the whole week, and a "Filtered: N of M entries" line offers Clear.
