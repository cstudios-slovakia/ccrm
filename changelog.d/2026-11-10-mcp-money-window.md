- **MCP: "what changed" and "what is due" for projects, invoices, expenses and the ledger** (`public/api/mcp_lists_money.php`, `mcp.php`, `mcp_ext.php`, `mcp_ext_intel.php`): `list_projects`, `list_invoices`, `list_expenses` and `list_financial_movements` return `created_at` / `updated_at` as ISO moments and take `created_from/to`, `updated_from/to`, `sort_by`, `sort_order`, `limit` and `offset` (an id tiebreak keeps pages stable). `get_project`, `get_invoice` and the milestone tasks return ISO moments too. `get_financial_mode` and `get_financial_simplified_table` return `updated_at`.
- **MCP: deadlines and due dates are queryable** (`list_projects`, `list_milestones`, `list_expenses`, `list_financial_movements`): `list_projects` takes `deadline_from/to`, `invoice_date_from/to` and a `bucket` (overdue, today, tomorrow, upcoming, done; open = not archived and not completed or cancelled). `list_milestones` works without `project_id` across all projects with the same ranges and bucket, plus `limit` and `offset`. `list_expenses` validates its dates and takes `due_from/to`. `list_financial_movements` takes `due_from/to`, `paid_from/to` and `issue_from/to`.
