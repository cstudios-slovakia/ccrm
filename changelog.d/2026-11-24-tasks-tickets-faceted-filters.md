- **Tasks and Tickets filters adjust to each other** (`TaskDashboardView.tsx`, `TicketsView.tsx`, `tasks/useTaskFacets.ts`, `tickets/useTicketFacets.ts`): client, project, assignee, assigner, priority and status on Tasks, and status, priority, project and assignee on Tickets, are multi-select dropdowns with select all / clear. Each option shows how many rows it would give with the other filters applied, and options the selection rules out are faded. The status dropdowns can pin options as chips above the list. On Tickets the KPI cards stay presets for "no status picked": picking a status suspends the card, clicking a card clears the status selection.
